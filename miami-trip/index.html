  • 9:00 AM

    Ease in with a Cuban coffee and eggs near the water. Fort Lauderdale Beach or Hollywood Beach Broadwalk are both relaxed, walkable starts to the day.
  • 11:00 AM

  • 1:30 PM

    Eat a proper meal before you head in — stadium queues are long. Grab Cuban (a media noche or ropa vieja) or something hearty. Hydrate hard; July in Miami is hot and humid.
  • Afternoon
    ⚽ England vs France — Hard Rock Stadium
    Arrive 2+ hours early. Security and gates for a World Cup match are serious — bring a clear bag, screenshot your mobile ticket, and wear your colours. Soak in the pre-match atmosphere; a bronze-final derby between these two is a bucket-list watch.
  • Full time
    The final whistle
    Let the crowd thin for ~30 minutes before leaving the lot — traffic out of Miami Gardens is heavy. Grab water and take it slow.
  • Evening
    Celebrate in the city
    Ride the high into the night. Brickell for rooftop bars and dinner, Wynwood for a younger crowd and breweries, or South Beach / Ocean Drive for the classic Miami neon walk. Pace yourself — you fly tomorrow.

Eat & explore

Wynwood Walls

Open-air street-art museum — murals, galleries, breweries and great coffee. The most photogenic corner of Miami.

🌆

Brickell

Miami's glossy downtown — rooftop bars, waterfront dining, and Brickell City Centre for a polished night out.

🏖️

South Beach

Pastel Art Deco hotels, Ocean Drive neon, and turquoise water. The classic Miami postcard.

☕

Little Havana

Calle Ocho, cafecito windows, cigar rollers and Cuban food. Order a media noche and a colada to share.

🌴

Las Olas

Fort Lauderdale's walkable dining strip — closest lively nightlife to your base if you stay up north.

🚤

Bayside & the water

Boat tours, waterfront eats, and skyline views. An easy, breezy way to see Miami from the sea.

Know Before You Go

Match-weekend playbook

🎟️

Ticket & bag rules

Mobile ticket screenshotted and charged. Clear-bag policy at Hard Rock — leave the backpack, bring only essentials.

🚗

Getting to the stadium

Hard Rock is ~20 min from FLL, ~25 from downtown Miami. Rideshare surges hard post-match — consider pre-booking or walking a few blocks out before ordering.

🥵

Beat the heat

July in Miami is 90°F+ and humid. Sunscreen, a hat, and constant water. Afternoon thunderstorms are common — check radar.

🚆

Brightline option

The Brightline train links Fort Lauderdale, Aventura and Miami if you'd rather skip driving between the two downtowns.

🏨

Where to stay

Aventura or Hollywood put you between FLL and the stadium. South Beach is more fun but a longer haul on match day.
